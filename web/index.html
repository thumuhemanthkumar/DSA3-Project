Similarity Detection

Exact phrases and overall lyrical overlap are solved with two different techniques, then combined into a single ranked list against your catalogue.

Knuth–Morris–Pratt matching

Finds every occurrence of a candidate phrase inside a catalogue song without rescanning characters it has already matched.

Cosine & Jaccard similarity

Each song becomes a set of words. These measures score how much two sets overlap, independent of word order.

Jaccard = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|
Cosine = (A · B) / (|A||B|)
A, B = token sets of the two songs
